I'm for sure going to post some stuff from [\#AllThingsOpen](<https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23allthingsopen&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED>) this week, but that got sideswiped yesterday/today for news from\.\.\. PHP? That wasn't on my bingo card\.

I bet it wasn't on most people's, either\. And in a day where exploits are appearing that are likely a decade old, it's never more important than ever to keep your software and systems up\-to\-date\.

This particular exploit was \- to no one's surprise \- found with the assistance of AI agents\. And it's a doozy, offering full access to the affected server\. That can mean reading application data, modifying the site, stealing credentials, passwords, tokens, API keys\. Yikes\.

Software doesn't need to have a change for its risk profile to change\. It requires maintenance and attention\. I expect we'll see lots of these popping up in the future\.

Our team was on top of this for all of our clients\. Come talk to me if yours needed a reminder\.
