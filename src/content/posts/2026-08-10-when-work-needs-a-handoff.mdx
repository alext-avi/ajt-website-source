Mostly agree\.

But sometimes the most efficient thing to do is hand this off to someone else/ their agents\. Sometimes my developers get instructions about the infrastructure or devops pipelines they don’t have the context / can’t digest\. I don’t want that knowledge through their filter\. For those, have Claude make a “handoff” markdown, and send me that with a note saying you don’t understand\.

For everything else, please digest it and give me just the part where I need to make a decision\. I’ve already got my own clauses verbose dialog to work through\. I don’t want yours\.
