Conferences Hack Day, a one\-day hackathon to take an idea through to a working demo\. We were encouraged to build with the sponsors tools\.

We used the day to explore an idea Ive been thinking about: making an agent \- your agent\- the primary interface to an application\. Food tracking at home was our test case\. We called it Buttery\.

You work through the assistant you already use: send it a receipt, confirm what to add, and it updates a record that survives the conversation\. The inventory lives in the application, so a new conversation doesnt mean starting over\. Build your skills and agents around it, and Buttery provides the essential tools/structure to manage the data\.

We kept an intentionally small UI to establish ground truth: see whats stored, check the evidence, and correct mistakes\.

Integrating the sponsors product was the name of the game, so we used [Similarweb](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/similarweb/>) for market research, [Crusoe](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/crusoe/>) running DeepSeek for receipt jargon interpretation, [Vultr](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/vultr/>) for hosting, and [DuploCloud](<https://www.linkedin.com/company/duplocloud/>) for a QA agent working through the same interface\.
