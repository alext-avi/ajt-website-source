## The challenge

A SaaS platform has to serve current customers while continuing to change. Product priorities, customer commitments, release reliability, and the long-term architecture all compete for the same engineering capacity.

My work at MealTrack connects those responsibilities rather than treating the roadmap and the technical platform as separate conversations.

## My role

As CTO and Product Owner, I balance product direction, architecture, customer commitments, team leadership, and hands-on technical oversight.

I led a cross-functional team of approximately 10 through modernization of a legacy platform into a product backed by React, GraphQL, Azure, and SQL Server.

## Approach and architecture

The modernization work supported a multi-tenant SaaS ecommerce platform. It combined frontend development, an API layer, cloud infrastructure, and a SQL Server data foundation.

The goal was to align the platform with customer growth and business priorities while maintaining a sustainable delivery process. Technical scope and product commitments needed to be considered together.

## Outcomes

My documented work includes improvements to release cadence, operational reliability, scalability, white-label capability, and platform governance.

This overview omits internal diagrams, customer-specific information, and performance figures that are not supported by public evidence.

## What this work illustrates

Platform leadership is partly architecture and partly judgment. A technical roadmap has to account for what customers need now, what the business can support, and what the team can responsibly deliver next.
