## About the talk

A product can be useful even when the person directing the work never opens its interface. This lightning talk introduces that shift through examples of agents working across software.

The discussion includes presentation creation, Blender, and call debriefs. The question for a product team is concrete: what should a user’s agent be able to do, and what evidence should it leave behind?
